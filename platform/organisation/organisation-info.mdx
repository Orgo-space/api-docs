This is your organization's identity card. Everything here — name, country, contact info, financial details — flows into invoices, emails, registration pages, and the mobile app.

**Settings → Organization → Organization Info**

<img src="/images/platform/organisation/org-info-basic-details.png" alt="Organization info form with name, country, and details" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", border: "1px solid var(--border-color)", marginBottom: "1rem" }} />

<Note>
  Requires **Tenant Admin** permissions.
</Note>

---

## Basic details

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Name & Short Name" icon="text">
    **Name** is your display name (required). **Short Name** is the abbreviated version for mobile headers and compact UI.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Country & Timezone" icon="globe">
    Sets regional defaults and how dates/times display. Toggle **Is international** if you operate across multiple countries.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Choose a **Default language** — this sets the platform language for new members and public pages. Available: English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Romanian, Slovak, Ukrainian, Bulgarian.

<Tip>
  Your workspace identifier appears as a badge next to the save button (e.g., `myorg-42`). The slug is auto-generated from your organization name.
</Tip>

## Contact information

![Contact information card with office email, phone, and address fields](/images/platform/organisation/org-info-contact.png)

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Office Email** | Required. Used in automated communications |
| **Office Phone** | Select country code first, then enter the number |
| **Office Address** | Your physical office location |

Below the contact card: **Privacy/GDPR URL** and **Terms of Service URL**. Both are shown during registration — make sure they're publicly accessible.

---

## Social media

![Social media URLs card with fields for Facebook, X.com, Youtube, LinkedIn, Instagram, and TikTok](/images/platform/organisation/org-info-social-media.png)

Paste the full URL for each platform you use. Leave the rest blank. These appear on your organization's public profile.

## Financial details

![Financial details card with currency, legal name, registration number, VAT, and IBAN fields](/images/platform/organisation/org-info-financial.png)

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Currency & Legal" icon="building-columns">
    **Currency** auto-fills the symbol. Add your **Legal Name**, **Registration Number**, and **VAT Number** for invoices and receipts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bank IBANs" icon="money-bill-transfer">
    One IBAN field for your primary currency, plus optional fields for **EUR**, **USD**, and **CHF** to accept multi-currency transfers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Get these right before enabling payments or membership fees — they appear on every invoice and donation receipt.
</Warning>

## Saving

![Save button with organization name and slug identifier](/images/platform/organisation/org-info-save-button.png)

The **Save** button sits in a sticky panel on the right — it follows you as you scroll. Hit it when you're done.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Phone number field is disabled">
    Select a country code from the dropdown first. The phone input activates once a country is selected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Currency symbol didn't update">
    It auto-updates when you change the currency dropdown. You can manually override it in the **Symbol** field if needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Changes aren't saving">
    **Name** and **Office Email** are required. Make sure both are filled in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Missing IBAN fields for certain currencies">
    IBAN fields appear dynamically based on your primary currency, plus EUR, USD, and CHF.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
