The Organisation Info page is where you define the core identity of your organization in Orgo. This information is used throughout the platform - in headers, footers, emails, and member communications.

## How to access

**Settings** → **Organisation** → **Info Organisatie**

## Basic Information

### Organisation Name

<ParamField path="name" type="string" required>
  The full official name of your organization. This appears in the platform header, emails, and official documents.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="shortName" type="string">
  A shortened version of your organization name. Used in places where space is limited, such as mobile navigation or compact headers.
</ParamField>

**Example:**
- Name: "International Scout Federation of Romania"
- Short Name: "ISFR"

### Location Settings

<ParamField path="country" type="select" required>
  Select the country where your organization is headquartered. This affects default timezone suggestions and regional formatting.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="timezone" type="select" required>
  Your organization's primary timezone. All event times, scheduled emails, and date displays will use this timezone by default.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="isInternational" type="boolean">
  Enable this if your organization operates across multiple countries. When enabled, the country field label changes to "Country Headquarter" to indicate it's the primary location.
</ParamField>

### Default Language

<ParamField path="language" type="select" required>
  The default language for your Orgo workspace. New members will see the platform in this language by default.

  **Available languages:** English, Romanian, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Ukrainian, Bulgarian, Dutch
</ParamField>

<Note>
  Members can change their personal language preference in their profile settings. The default language applies to new members and public pages.
</Note>

## Contact Information

### Office Contact

<ParamField path="contactEmail" type="email" required>
  The primary email address for your organization. This is displayed to members and used as the reply-to address for automated emails.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="contactPhone" type="phone">
  Your organization's phone number. Select the country code first, then enter the phone number.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="officeAddress" type="string">
  The physical address of your organization's office. Displayed in the footer and on contact pages.
</ParamField>

## Legal & Compliance

### Compliance URLs

<ParamField path="urlGDPR" type="url">
  Link to your organization's Privacy Policy or GDPR compliance page. This link is displayed in the platform footer and during user registration.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="urlTerms" type="url">
  Link to your Terms of Service for members. Displayed during registration and in the platform footer.
</ParamField>

<Warning>
  If you collect personal data from EU residents, you are required to have a Privacy Policy. Make sure these URLs point to valid, accessible pages.
</Warning>

### Legal Information

<ParamField path="legalNumber" type="string">
  Your organization's legal registration number (e.g., company registration number, nonprofit ID, VAT number).
</ParamField>

## Financial Settings

### Currency

<ParamField path="currency" type="select" required>
  The default currency for your organization. Used for membership fees, event tickets, products, and all financial transactions.

  Orgo supports 100+ currencies including EUR, USD, GBP, RON, CHF, and more.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="currencySymbol" type="string">
  The symbol displayed alongside prices (e.g., €, $, £, RON). This is automatically set when you select a currency but can be customized.
</ParamField>

### Bank Account Details

If you accept bank transfers for payments, add your IBAN details:

<ParamField path="bankIbanNative" type="string">
  Your bank account IBAN in your native currency.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="bankIbanEur" type="string">
  Your EUR bank account IBAN (if different from native currency).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="bankIbanUsd" type="string">
  Your USD bank account IBAN (if applicable).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="bankIbanChf" type="string">
  Your CHF bank account IBAN (if applicable).
</ParamField>

<Tip>
  Bank details are displayed to members when they choose to pay via bank transfer. Make sure these are accurate to avoid payment issues.
</Tip>

## Saving Changes

After making changes:

1. Click the **Save** button in the top right corner
2. Wait for the confirmation message
3. Changes take effect immediately across the platform
