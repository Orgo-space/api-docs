The Organisation section is your starting point for setting up Orgo. Here you define your organization's identity, visual branding, and which platform features are available to your members.

## What you can configure

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Organisation Info" icon="building" href="/platform/organisation/organisation-info">
    Set your organization name, contact details, location, currency, and legal information
  </Card>
  <Card title="Branding" icon="palette" href="/platform/organisation/branding">
    Upload logos for light and dark themes, and customize the login page background
  </Card>
  <Card title="Modules" icon="grid-2" href="/platform/organisation/modules">
    Enable or disable platform features like Events, Payments, Discussions, and more
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Getting started

When you first create your Orgo workspace, we recommend completing these steps in order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Organisation Info">
    Add your organization name, country, timezone, and contact details. This information appears across the platform and in communications with members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logos">
    Add your organization logo in both light and dark versions. A square icon version is also recommended for mobile apps and favicons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure modules">
    Review the available modules and enable only the features your organization needs. You can always enable more modules later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your first members">
    Once your basic setup is complete, start inviting administrators and members to join your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quick tips

<Tip>
  Keep your organisation info up to date. Changes to your organization name and logo will be reflected across the entire platform, including emails sent to members.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Only users with the **Admin Tenant** role can access Organisation settings. Make sure you have the appropriate permissions before trying to access these pages.
</Note>

## Navigation

In the Orgo platform, you can access Organisation settings from:

**Settings menu** (gear icon) → **Organisation**

From there, you'll find:
- Info Organisatie
- Branding
- Module
