<Note>
  Requires **Tenant Admin** permissions. Don't see the gear icon? Ask your admin.
</Note>

## Forms

Forms are configured inside the **Users & Profiles** module. Three form types, same drag-and-drop builder:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Registration Form" icon="user-plus">
    What new members fill in when they sign up
  </Card>
  <Card title="Profile Form" icon="address-card">
    What appears on a member's profile after registration
  </Card>
  <Card title="Adhesion Form" icon="file-signature">
    A separate application form for prospective members
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**Settings → Modules → Users & Profiles → Registration Form**

![Registration form builder with available fields on the left and form layout on the right](/images/platform/organisation/registration-form-fields.png)

Drag fields from the left panel into your form on the right. Mark which ones are required. Reorder however you like.

- **Email** is always included — can't remove it
- Switch between **System Fields** and **Custom Fields** tabs
- Add a title and intro text in **Form Settings** below the fields
- Click **Iframe** to get an embeddable link for your website

<Note>
  File upload fields only appear on the profile form — they require authentication, so they're excluded from registration.
</Note>

## Email templates

**Settings → Modules → Emails & Notifications → Templates**

Every automated email — welcome messages, event reminders, payment receipts — has a customizable template, organized by category.

![Email templates page with category sidebar and template cards](/images/platform/organisation/email-templates-grid.png)

Click any card to open the editor. Yellow **Disabled** tag = that email won't be sent until you enable it.

<Tip>
  Missing a category? It means the related module isn't active. Enable it under **Modules** and the category appears.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't see Organization Settings">
    You need the **Tenant Admin** role. Ask your organization's admin to grant it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Some email template categories are missing">
    Categories are tied to active modules. Enable the module, come back — the category appears.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Required profile fields aren't prompting members">
    The prompt triggers after login, not during registration. Check both the **visible** and **required** columns on the Profile Form page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
