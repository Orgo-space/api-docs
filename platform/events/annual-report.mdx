---

The Annual Event Report aggregates data from all events in a given year, providing statistics, trends, and summaries for organizational reporting.

## How to access

**Events** → **Annual Report** (if enabled)

Or: **Settings** → **Events** → **Reports**

---

## Prerequisites

Annual Reports must be enabled in module settings:

**Settings** → **Events** → **Module Settings**:
- **Enable Annual Event Reports** - Master toggle
- **National Annual Report** - Organization-wide reports
- **Local Annual Report** - Reports per local center (if local centers enabled)

---

## What's Included

### Event Statistics

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Total Events** | Number of events held |
| **By Type** | Breakdown by event type |
| **Total Attendance** | Sum of all attendees |
| **Unique Attendees** | Individual people who attended |
| **Average Attendance** | Per-event average |
| **Attendance Rate** | Check-ins vs registrations |

### Trends

- Events per month
- Attendance over time
- Growth vs previous year
- Most popular event types

### Breakdowns

- By event type
- By organizer/group
- By local center (if applicable)
- By SDG alignment (if enabled)

## Generating Reports

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Annual Report">
    Go to Events → Annual Report
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select year">
    Choose which year to generate the report for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure options">
    Select which data to include (by type, location, etc.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click Generate to create the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Download as PDF or share online.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Report Types

### National Annual Report

Organization-wide summary:
- All events across all locations
- Aggregated statistics
- Year-over-year comparison
- Impact metrics

### Local Annual Report

Per-local-center breakdown:
- Events organized by the local center
- Local attendance figures
- Comparison to other centers
- Local volunteer hours

<Note>
  Local Annual Report is only available if the Local Centers module is enabled.
</Note>

---

## SDG Impact Tracking

If SDG tagging is enabled, annual reports can include:

- Events by SDG goal
- Impact alignment percentage
- SDG trend over years
- Contribution to specific goals

This is valuable for:
- Grant reporting
- Sustainability documentation
- UN reporting (for applicable organizations)

---

## Volunteer Hours

If volunteer hour tracking is enabled:

- Total volunteer hours for the year
- Hours by event type
- Hours by member
- Year-over-year growth
