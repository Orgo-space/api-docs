Orgo is a membership management platform. Member records, chapters and groups, events, payments and communications sit in one system, so the people you email are the people you already manage, and a fee payment updates the profile it belongs to.

**Built for** membership organizations, especially ones with chapters or branches: youth organizations, professional associations, trade unions, alumni networks, faith communities, and advocacy groups.

**Replaces** spreadsheets, mailing-list software, and standalone event-registration tools. Works alongside Stripe, which handles the card payments and the payouts.

<Note>
  Orgo is modular. Most of what is listed below is a module you switch on in **Settings → Modules**, so your workspace only shows the parts you use. See [Modules](/platform/organisation/modules) for the full list and what each one turns on.
</Note>

## Set up your organization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in your organization record">
    Name, contact details, legal identity, currency and bank details. Invoices, emails and public pages all read from it.

    [Organization Info →](/platform/organisation/organisation-info) · [Branding →](/platform/organisation/branding)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the modules you need">
    Events, payments, discussions, campaigns, documents. Everything else stays hidden.

    [Modules →](/platform/organisation/modules)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide how people join">
    Design the registration form, define your user types, and choose whether new members need approval.

    [Registration Form →](/platform/users/registration-form) · [User Types & Roles →](/platform/users/user-types)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your structure">
    Chapters for geography, units for teams inside them, role groups that fill themselves from member roles.

    [Groups & Teams →](/platform/groups)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your team access">
    Assign permissions by domain and scope so chapter admins see their chapter and nobody shares a login.

    [Assigning Permissions →](/platform/permissions-assigning)
  </Step>
</Steps>
