## First time here? Start with setup.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure your organization">
    Set your name, logo, branding, and enable the modules you need. Takes 5 minutes.

    [Organisation Setup →](/platform/organisation)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up how members join">
    Design your registration form, create user types, and decide whether new members need approval.

    [Users & Profiles →](/platform/users)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize your structure">
    Create groups for geographic chapters, automatic role-based lists, and project teams.

    [Groups & Teams →](/platform/groups)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start engaging">
    Create your first event, send a newsletter, or open discussions. You're live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Orgo does

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Members" icon="users" href="/platform/users">
    Registration, profiles, roles, permissions, family accounts, and the full member lifecycle from joining to departure.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Events" icon="calendar" href="/platform/events">
    Create events, sell tickets via Stripe, check in with QR codes, and generate impact reports.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payments" icon="credit-card" href="/platform/fees/stripe-integration">
    Membership fees, product sales, donations with progress tracking, and local center billing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Communication" icon="comments" href="/platform/discussion">
    Forum discussions, email newsletters with drag-and-drop builder, and push notifications.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Groups" icon="users-rectangle" href="/platform/groups">
    Geographic branches, automatic role groups, and private project teams — each with their own space.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Governance" icon="landmark" href="/platform/organisational-chart">
    Org chart, e-voting for elections, and an official gazette for formal records.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Documents" icon="file-signature" href="/platform/contracts">
    Digital contracts with e-signatures, shared file storage, and folder management.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Learning" icon="graduation-cap" href="/platform/courses">
    Course management with lessons, quizzes, and automatic badge awards on completion.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Customization" icon="palette" href="/platform/customization">
    Custom menus, dashboard layouts, multilingual support, and your own branding everywhere.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Built for these organizations

| Type | How Orgo helps |
|------|---------------|
| **Youth scouting** | Family accounts, age restrictions, local chapters, badge progression, volunteer hour tracking |
| **Professional associations** | Identity verification, membership applications, credential management, CPD tracking |
| **Federations & unions** | Local centers with delegated admin, regional hierarchy, org-wide announcements, e-voting |
| **Nonprofits & NGOs** | Donation pages, SDG impact tagging, annual reports, volunteer management |
| **Clubs & communities** | Simple registration, event RSVPs, group discussions, shared files |
