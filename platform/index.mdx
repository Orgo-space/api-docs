Orgo is a membership management platform that unifies member records, events, payments, and communications in one connected system.

**Built for:** membership organizations with multiple chapters, including youth organizations, professional associations, trade unions, and advocacy groups, and similar member-based groups.

**Replaces:** spreadsheets, mailing-list software, and standalone event-registration tools for day-to-day membership management. Works alongside Stripe, syncing directly for payment processing and payouts.

## Built for these organizations

| Type | How Orgo helps |
|------|---------------|
| **Youth organizations** | Family accounts, age restrictions, local chapters, badge progression, volunteer hour tracking |
| **Professional associations** | [Identity verification](/platform/identity-validation), membership applications, credential management, CPD tracking |
| **Federations & unions** | Local centers with delegated admin, regional hierarchy, org-wide announcements, e-voting |
| **Political parties** | E-voting for internal elections, local centers for regional branches, donation tracking, official record-keeping |
| **Nonprofits & NGOs** | Donation pages, SDG impact tagging, annual reports, volunteer management |
| **Clubs & communities** | Simple registration, event RSVPs, group discussions, shared files |
