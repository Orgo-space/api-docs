Local Centers (also called Local Groups or Chapters) represent geographic divisions of your organization. They allow members to connect and collaborate with others in their area while maintaining connection to the national organization.

## How to access

**Sidebar** → **Local Centers**

Or: **Settings** → **Local Centers** → **Module Settings**

---

## What are Local Centers

Local Centers provide:

- **Geographic organization** - Divide your organization by location
- **Local autonomy** - Each center has its own discussions, events, and files
- **Local administration** - Dedicated admins manage local activities
- **Member assignment** - Members belong to their local chapter

---

## Local Center Structure

### Hierarchy

```
National Organization (Tenant)
├── Region A
│   ├── Local Center 1
│   ├── Local Center 2
│   └── Local Center 3
└── Region B
    ├── Local Center 4
    └── Local Center 5
```

### Center Properties

Each Local Center has:

| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Name** | Display name for the center |
| **Town/Location** | Geographic location |
| **Region** | Parent region (if regions enabled) |
| **Status** | Active, Pending Close, Suspended, or Closed |
| **Contact Info** | Email, phone, contact person |
| **Address** | Office and postal addresses |
| **Social Links** | Facebook, Instagram, Twitter |

---

## Creating a Local Center

**Settings** → **Local Centers** → **Create Local Center**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Basic Information">
    Enter name, select town/location, and choose region if applicable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Details">
    Add contact email, phone, and contact person name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Addresses">
    Enter office address and postal address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Additional Settings">
    Configure bank details, social media links, and map location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save to create the Local Center.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Local Center Settings

### Basic Information

<ParamField path="name" type="string" required>
  Display name for the Local Center.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="alias" type="string">
  Short alias or abbreviation.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="town" type="relation">
  Geographic location (town/city).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="region" type="relation">

</ParamField>

### Contact Information

<ParamField path="contactEmail" type="email">
  Public contact email for the center.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="contactPhone" type="phone">
  Public contact phone number.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="contactFullName" type="string">
  Name of primary contact person.
</ParamField>

### Addresses

<ParamField path="officeAddress" type="string">
  Physical office/meeting location.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="postalAddress" type="text">
  Mailing address for correspondence.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="geoLocation" type="string">
  GPS coordinates for map display.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="mapDescription" type="text">
  Description shown on public map.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="facebookUrl" type="url">

<ParamField path="instagramUrl" type="url">
  Instagram profile URL.

## Local Center Statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Active** | Fully operational center |
| **Pending Close** | Center scheduled for closure |
| **Suspended** | Temporarily inactive |
| **Closed** | Permanently closed |

## Local Center Features

Each Local Center functions as a group with:

### Discussions
- Local announcements and conversations
- Topics relevant to the geographic area
- Local community engagement

### Events
- Local meetups and activities
- Events specific to the area
- Local calendar management

### Files
- Local documents and resources
- Center-specific materials
- Administrative documents

### Members
- Members assigned to this center
- Local leadership and roles
- Member management

---

## Local Administration

### Local Admin Roles

- **ADMIN_LOCAL** - Full administrative access to the local center
- **HR_LOCAL** - Member management within the local center
- **FINANCIAL_LOCAL** - Financial management for the local center

### What Local Admins Can Do

- Manage local discussions, events, and files
- Edit center information
- Manage local members
- View local reports
- Process local fees (if enabled)

## Waitlist Management

Local Centers can have capacity limits:

<ParamField path="waitlistCapacity" type="number">
  Maximum members for this center. New registrations go to waitlist when full.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="waitlistAutoApprove" type="boolean">
  Automatically approve waitlist members when capacity opens.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="waitlistProcessingRate" type="number">
  Rate at which waitlist members are processed.
</ParamField>

## Public Map

If enabled, Local Centers appear on a public map:

- Shows location of all active centers
- Displays contact information
- Helps prospective members find local chapters
- Customizable map descriptions
