Orgo uses Stripe to process all online payments. Connect your Stripe account to start accepting credit cards, bank transfers, and other payment methods.

## How to access

**Settings** → **Fees** → **Stripe Integration**

---

## Prerequisites

Before connecting Stripe:

1. Create a Stripe account at [stripe.com](https://stripe.com)
2. Complete Stripe's identity verification process
3. Have your organization's banking details ready

---

## Connecting Stripe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Stripe settings">
    Go to **Settings** → **Fees** → **Stripe Integration**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect Stripe">
    Click the **Connect with Stripe** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to Stripe">
    You'll be redirected to Stripe. Log in or create an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize connection">
    Authorize Orgo to connect to your Stripe account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete verification">
    Complete any required verification steps in Stripe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Orgo">
    You'll be redirected back to Orgo with Stripe connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Stripe Connect Modes

Orgo uses Stripe Connect, which supports different account types:

| Mode | Description | Best For |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Standard** | Member creates their own Stripe account | Organizations that want full Stripe dashboard access |
| **Express** | Simplified onboarding through Orgo | Quick setup with less administrative overhead |

---

## What's Connected

When you connect Stripe, Orgo can:

- Create payment intents and process charges
- Create and manage subscriptions
- Issue refunds
- Create products and prices in Stripe
- Access payment history and reports

---

## Stripe Account Status

After connecting, check your account status:

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Active** | Ready to accept payments |
| **Pending** | Awaiting verification completion |
| **Restricted** | Some features limited - complete verification |
| **Disabled** | Account disabled - contact Stripe support |

## Payment Methods

Stripe supports various payment methods:

### Credit/Debit Cards
- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- Other local cards

### Bank Payments
- SEPA Direct Debit (Europe)
- Bank transfers
- iDEAL (Netherlands)
- Bancontact (Belgium)

### Digital Wallets
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- Link (Stripe's one-click checkout)

<Note>
Available payment methods depend on your Stripe account country and settings.
</Note>

## Fees and Pricing

### Stripe Fees

Stripe charges per transaction:
- ~2.9% + €0.25 for card payments (varies by country)
- Lower fees for SEPA/bank payments
- See [Stripe Pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing) for current rates

### Orgo Platform Fee

Depending on your Orgo plan:
- Additional platform fee may apply
- Check your subscription agreement for details

---

## Payouts

Stripe transfers funds to your bank account:

### Payout Schedule

Configure in Stripe Dashboard:
- Daily (default in most countries)
- Weekly
- Monthly
- Manual

### Payout Timing

Funds are typically available:
- 2 business days (most countries)
- 7+ days for new accounts or high-risk transactions

## Testing with Stripe

### Test Mode

Use Stripe's test mode for development:

1. Enable test mode in Stripe Dashboard
2. Use test card numbers:
   - `4242 4242 4242 4242` - Successful payment
   - `4000 0000 0000 0002` - Card declined
3. Test subscriptions and refunds safely

<Warning>
Never use real card numbers in test mode or test cards in live mode.
</Warning>

## Local Center Stripe Accounts

For multi-location organizations:

### Connected Accounts

Each Local Center can have its own Stripe account:

1. Local Center admin connects their Stripe account
2. Payments for local products go to local account
3. National organization can track all payments

### Fee Distribution

Configure how fees are split:
- 100% to Local Center
- Split between national and local
- National collects, distributes to local

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Stripe not connecting">
    Check that your browser allows popups. Try a different browser if issues persist.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Payments failing">
    Check Stripe Dashboard for error details. Common issues:
    - Card declined by bank
    - 3D Secure authentication failed
    - Insufficient funds
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Payouts not arriving">
    Verify bank details in Stripe Dashboard. Check payout schedule settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Account restricted">
    Complete verification in Stripe Dashboard. Respond to any information requests.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Webhook errors">
    Contact Orgo support if webhook events aren't processing correctly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security

Stripe integration follows security best practices:

- **PCI Compliance** - Card data never touches Orgo servers
- **Encryption** - All communications encrypted with TLS
- **Tokenization** - Card details stored as secure tokens
- **3D Secure** - Strong customer authentication supported

---

## Disconnecting Stripe

To disconnect your Stripe account:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Fees** → **Stripe Integration**
2. Click **Disconnect**
3. Confirm disconnection

<Warning>
Disconnecting will:
- Stop all payment processing
- Cancel active subscriptions
- Remove payment methods

Export any needed data before disconnecting.
</Warning>
