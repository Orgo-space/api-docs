The Contacts module manages individuals who aren't platform members but have a relationship with your organization. Track donors, newsletter subscribers, event attendees, and other external contacts.

## How to access

**Sidebar** → **Contacts**

Or: **Resources** → **Contacts**

---

## What are Contacts

Contacts are external individuals who:

- Subscribe to newsletters
- Donate without creating accounts
- Attend public events
- Have business relationships with your organization
- Are potential future members

<Note>
Contacts are different from Members. Members have accounts and can log in. Contacts are external records.
</Note>

## Creating Contacts

### Manual Creation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Contacts">
    Go to **Contacts** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Contact">
    Click the **+ Create Contact** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in details">
    Enter name, email, and other relevant information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set preferences">
    Configure newsletter subscription and status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** to create the contact.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Automatic Creation

Contacts are automatically created when:
- Someone donates without an account
- Someone subscribes to your newsletter
- Someone registers for a public event
- Imported from external systems

## Newsletter Management

### Subscription Status

<ParamField path="isNewsletterSubscribed" type="boolean">
  Whether the contact is subscribed to newsletters.
</ParamField>

### Subscribing Contacts

1. Edit the contact
2. Toggle **Newsletter Subscription** on
3. Save

### Unsubscribing

Contacts can unsubscribe via:
- Link in newsletter emails
- Contact preferences
- Admin manually updating

<Warning>
Always honor unsubscribe requests promptly. This is legally required in most jurisdictions.
</Warning>

## Import & Export

### Importing Contacts

Import contacts from CSV files:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare CSV">
    Create CSV with columns: name, email, phone, country, town, etc.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Import">
    Go to **Contacts** → **Import**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload CSV">
    Select your CSV file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map fields">
    Match CSV columns to contact fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and import">
    Preview the import and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Exporting Contacts

Export contacts for external use:

1. Go to **Contacts**
2. Apply filters if needed
3. Click **Export**
4. Download CSV file

## Converting Contacts to Members

When a contact wants to become a member:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the contact">
    Search for the contact in the Contacts list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Account">
    Use the **Create Account** action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete registration">
    Fill in additional member details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite">
    Send invitation to complete account setup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The contact record links to the new member account.
