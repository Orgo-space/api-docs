Every automatic email ships with a written-for-you version in your organisation's
language. When that wording does not sound like you, change it: click a sentence in
the email and type your own. The design stays Orgo's, so it keeps up with later
improvements; only your words are saved.

**Built for** organisations whose voice matters. **Replaces** apologising for the
robot that emailed your members.

Everything lives on one page: **Settings** → **Notifications & Email Campaign** →
**Emails** (`/tenants/modules/emails/templates`). Admins (ADMIN_TENANT) and
communication managers (COMMUNICATION_TENANT) can open it and edit wording;
communication managers reach the same page from the main menu at
`/email-templates`. Only admins can switch emails on or off, see
[System emails](/platform/emails/system-emails).

## Finding an email

The page lists every email your organisation sends, one card per section:
**Users**, **Referrals**, **Adhesion**, **Membership & Fees**, **Identity**,
**eDocuments**, **Events**, **Donations**, **Subscriptions**, **Admin alerts**,
**Sign-in & security** and **Activity notifications**.

- The sections also appear under **Emails** in the left menu. Clicking one scrolls
  the page to it.
- **Search emails** matches names and descriptions.
- The filters narrow the list to **Editable** emails, those that **Can be turned
  off**, or those that are **Off**. Each filter shows how many emails it holds.

Only sections that apply to you appear: **Referrals** and **Adhesion** show when
those features are switched on, and **Membership & Fees**, **Identity**,
**eDocuments**, **Events**, **Donations** and **Subscriptions** disappear when the
matching feature is off. The referral emails also follow your registration
setting: with manual approval on you see the versions that mention approval,
without it the ones that do not.

Emails Orgo sends to your organisation about its own Orgo subscription, and
internal messages such as "your report is ready", are not listed: they are not
yours to change.

## Previewing an email

Click any row to open a preview of the email as your members receive it, with
your wording, your logo and colour, and your own account's details filled in.
This works for every email on the page, including the ones you cannot edit or
switch off. The subject line sits above the email.

Sign-in codes show a sample code. **Activity notifications** (new discussions,
comments, mentions, votes and the discussion summary) show sample content in your
organisation's language.

## Changing the wording

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Click **Edit text** on the row, or **Edit text** in the preview. The email
    opens exactly as it is sent, with every text you can change outlined.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a text and type">
    A heading or a button label is plain text: **Enter** keeps your change,
    **Escape** puts the previous wording back. A paragraph can also take
    **bold**, *italic* and links, from the buttons above the email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert placeholders">
    Click where the value should go, then pick it from **Insert Placeholder**. The
    menu lists only what this email can fill in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the subject">
    The **Subject** field sits above the email. Leave it alone and members keep
    getting the default subject in your language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    **Preview** shows the email with your changes and your account's details. **Back
    to Editor** returns to editing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test">
    **Send Test Email** sends the email with your unsaved changes to your own
    address, with `[TEST]` in front of the subject. It saves nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Save** makes your wording the one members receive from that moment on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<img src="/images/platform/emails/email-template-editor.png" alt="Inline editor for the Welcome email: the Subject field, the Insert Placeholder and Use default buttons, and the email itself with each editable text outlined, with Send Test Email, Preview and Save in the header" style={{ width: "100%", borderRadius: "8px", border: "1px solid var(--border-color)", marginBottom: "1rem" }} />

To put one text back to the default, clear it and save. Every text you leave
untouched keeps the default wording, so a later improvement to that sentence still
reaches your members.

## Placeholders

A placeholder is written `{{name}}` and is replaced with real data when the email
is sent. Most emails can use these:

| Placeholder | Becomes |
|---|---|
| `{{firstName}}`, `{{lastName}}`, `{{fullName}}` | The recipient's name |
| `{{userEmail}}` | The recipient's address |
| `{{userPhoneNumber}}`, `{{userAddress}}`, `{{userTown}}`, `{{userCountry}}` | Their contact details |
| `{{localCenterName}}` | Their local centre |
| `{{companyName}}` | Their company, when the Companies feature is on |
| `{{membershipLevelName}}`, `{{membershipLevelPrice}}` | Their membership tier and its price |
| `{{subscriptionEndDate}}` | The date their membership runs to |
| `{{organizationName}}`, `{{organizationLogo}}` | Your organisation's name and logo image |
| `{{organizationHost}}`, `{{platformLink}}` | Your Orgo address |
| `{{organizationCurrency}}`, `{{organizationTimezone}}`, `{{organizationLanguage}}` | Your organisation's settings |
| `{{contactEmail}}` | Your organisation's contact address |
| `{{profileLink}}` | A link to the recipient's profile |
| `{{paymentLink}}` | A link that takes the member straight to paying their membership |
| `{{contractsLink}}` | A link to the recipient's documents |
| `{{unsubscribeUrl}}`, `{{preferencesUrl}}` | Unsubscribe and notification preference links |

Individual emails add their own: event emails carry the event's name, date and
place, contract reminders the document and its expiry date, renewal reminders the
expiry date and the product. The **Insert Placeholder** menu is the authoritative
list for the email you have open.

<Note>
**Save** refuses a placeholder the email cannot fill and names it, so a typo such
as `{{firstname}}` cannot reach your members as literal text. Pick a replacement
from **Insert Placeholder**.
</Note>

## Going back to the original

**Use default** in the editor toolbar removes your wording and your subject for this
email, after you confirm. It cannot be undone: the email goes back to the built-in
version exactly as it was before you touched it.

## Language

The built-in wording exists in English, Romanian, German, Bulgarian, Lithuanian,
Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Ukrainian and Portuguese. Orgo uses your
organisation's default language; any other language falls back to English.

Your wording is saved for the language you wrote it in. If you later change the
organisation's default language, members get the built-in wording in the new
language until you edit the email again.

The invoice email is not a template at all. It and the PDF it carries render in
your organisation's default language across all 14 interface languages, falling
back to English only when the default is not one of them. See
[Invoices](/platform/fees/invoices).

## Emails customised in the old editor

An email you customised before the inline editor arrived was saved as a whole
document. Members still receive that saved version, without the current design,
and the editor says so in a notice above the email. Saving the email in the editor
replaces it with the current design and your texts.

---

## What you cannot change

- **Sign-in & security** emails (login, multi-factor, verification, password and
  email-change codes and confirmations, and the event app code) can be previewed
  but not edited. They carry live one-time codes, so their wording is fixed.
- **Activity notifications** can be previewed but not edited. Each member chooses
  which ones they get, see [Notifications](/platform/notifications).
- A few admin alerts, such as **Reactivation request**, **Group transfer request**,
  **Resignation request** and **Referral received**, can be switched off but not
  edited.

Some emails have no row at all and always send in their built-in wording, among
them invoices, family invitations, waitlist messages, task notifications and
event-app networking messages.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My change did not appear in the email members got">
    Check that you pressed **Save** rather than only previewing, then check
    [Email logs](/platform/emails/email-log): the log stores the body that was
    actually sent, which settles it immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Save says the email cannot fill a placeholder">
    That value is not available to this email, often because of a typo or a
    placeholder copied from another email. Delete it and pick a replacement from
    **Insert Placeholder**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The email still has the old design">
    It was customised in the old editor. Open it in the editor and save: it moves
    to the current design and keeps your texts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The test email never arrived">
    Test sends go to the address on your own account and use the same sending path
    as everything else, so a missing test usually means a delivery problem rather
    than a template problem. Check your spam folder, then see
    [Emails](/platform/emails).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I want the original wording back but only for one sentence">
    Clear that text in the editor and save. To reset the whole email, use **Use
    default**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
