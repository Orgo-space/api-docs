---

The invitation system lets existing members invite new people to join your organization. You can control who can send invitations and track referral sources.

## How to access

**Settings** → **Users & Profiles** → **Users Module** → **User Invitations**

---

## Enabling Invitations

<ParamField path="hasUserInvitation" type="boolean">
  Toggle to enable or disable the invitation feature. When enabled, members can access the "Invite" functionality from their dashboard or profile menu.
</ParamField>

---

## Who Can Send Invitations

<ParamField path="whoCanSendUserInvitation" type="select">
  Control which permission level is required to send invitations:

  | Value | Who Can Invite |
  |-------|----------------|
  | **USER** | All members can invite |
  | **HR_LOCAL** | Only local HR and above |
  | **HR_TENANT** | Only organization HR and above |
  | **ADMIN_LOCAL** | Only local admins and above |
  | **ADMIN_TENANT** | Only organization admins |
</ParamField>

---

## How Invitations Work

### For the Inviter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access invite feature">
    Member clicks "Invite" from their menu or dashboard
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter details">
    Enters invitee's email and optional personal message
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invitation">
    Clicks send to deliver the invitation email
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track status">
    Can see pending and accepted invitations
  </Step>
</Steps>

### For the Invitee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive email">
    Gets invitation email with personalized message
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click link">
    Clicks the registration link in the email
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete registration">
    Fills out the registration form (linked to inviter)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join organization">
    Becomes a member with invitation tracked
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Invitation Tracking

All invitations are tracked in the system:

- **Pending invitations** - Sent but not yet accepted
- **Accepted invitations** - Invitee completed registration
- **Expired invitations** - Link expired without registration

Administrators can view invitation statistics and who invited whom.

---

## Invitation Email

The invitation email includes:

- Organization name and branding
- Inviter's name
- Personal message (if provided)
- Direct registration link
- Organization description

<Tip>
  Personalized invitations with a message from the inviter have higher conversion rates than generic invites.
</Tip>

## Use Cases

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Membership growth">
    Enable all members to invite, encouraging organic growth through personal networks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Controlled recruitment">
    Limit invitations to HR or admins when you want to carefully manage new member intake.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Referral programs">
    Track who invites whom to recognize top recruiters or reward successful referrals.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Event-based invitations">
    Allow members to invite guests to specific events (separate from full membership).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

---

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Set appropriate permissions">
    If your organization requires vetting of new members, restrict invitations to HR or admins.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Encourage personal messages">
    Remind members that personal messages increase acceptance rates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Monitor invitation quality">
    If you're getting low-quality registrations, consider restricting who can invite.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Combine with adhesion">
    Even if members can invite anyone, you can still require an adhesion (application) process for formal membership.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
