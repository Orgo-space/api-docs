---
title: 'Waitlist'

---

The Waitlist feature helps you manage overflow when your organization has limited membership capacity or wants to control new member intake.

## How to access

**Settings** → **Users & Profiles** → **Users Module** → **Waitlist Settings**

---

## Enabling Waitlist

<ParamField path="isActive" type="boolean">
  Enable the waitlist module. When active, users can join a waitlist when membership is full or closed.
</ParamField>

---

## Configuration Options

<ParamField path="allowSelfRemoval" type="boolean">
  When enabled, users can remove themselves from the waitlist. When disabled, only administrators can remove entries.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="notifyAdminsOnNew" type="boolean">
  Send email notifications to administrators when someone joins the waitlist.
</ParamField>

---

## How the Waitlist Works

### User Experience

<Steps>
  <Step title="User attempts to register">
    When membership is closed or full, user sees waitlist option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Joins waitlist">
    User provides their email and basic info to join the waitlist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    User receives confirmation that they're on the waitlist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notification when spot opens">
    When space becomes available, waitlisted users are notified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete registration">
    User can then complete full registration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Administrator Experience

<Steps>
  <Step title="View waitlist">
    Access the waitlist from the admin dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage entries">
    Review, prioritize, or remove waitlist entries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite from waitlist">
    When spots open, invite people from the waitlist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track conversions">
    Monitor how many waitlist members convert to full members.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Waitlist Priority

Administrators can manage waitlist priority:

- **First come, first served** - Default order by signup date
- **Manual prioritization** - Reorder entries based on criteria
- **Priority settings** - Configure rules for automatic prioritization

## Use Cases

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Capacity limits">
    Organizations with physical or legal membership limits can manage overflow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cohort-based membership">
    Organizations that accept new members in batches can collect interest between cohorts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Exclusive membership">
    Create scarcity and exclusivity by maintaining a waitlist.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Seasonal registration">
    Collect signups during off-seasons for next enrollment period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Demand measurement">
    Gauge interest before expanding capacity or opening registration.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Waitlist vs. Adhesion

| Feature | Waitlist | Adhesion |
|---------|----------|----------|
| **Purpose** | Manage capacity | Approve applicants |
| **When used** | Registration closed | Registration open |
| **Process** | Wait for spot | Application review |
| **User action** | Wait | Submit application |
| **Admin action** | Invite when ready | Approve/reject |

You can use both:
1. Waitlist when capacity is full
2. Adhesion process for those invited from waitlist

## Notifications

### For Users

- Confirmation of waitlist signup
- Position updates (optional)
- Invitation to register when spot opens

### For Administrators

- New waitlist entry (if enabled)
- Waitlist size alerts (configurable)

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Communicate wait times">
    Let waitlisted users know approximately how long they might wait.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Keep waitlist engaged">
    Periodically email waitlisted users with updates or content to maintain interest.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Set expectations">
    Be clear that being on the waitlist doesn't guarantee membership.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Clean up periodically">
    Remove entries that have been waiting too long without conversion.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Consider self-removal">
    Allowing self-removal keeps the list accurate and reduces admin work.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
