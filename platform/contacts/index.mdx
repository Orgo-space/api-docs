A contact is someone you hold data about. A member is someone who can log in. That single
line decides everything else in this section.

Both live in the same organisation, side by side. A contact has a record, an email address
and a history of what they paid, signed and attended. What a contact does not have is an
account: no password, no permissions, no groups, and no membership fee. A member has all of
that plus a login.

**Built for** organisations whose audience is wider than their membership: donors,
newsletter subscribers, event guests, partner staff, and people who have not joined yet.
**Replaces** a separate CRM, or a spreadsheet kept alongside the member list.

## Contact or member

|  | Contact | Member |
|---|---|---|
| **Login** | No account and no password. The one exception is the Event App, where a ticket holder signs in with a one-time code that works only there | Username and password, with optional two-factor |
| **Roles and permissions** | None. The **Role** field on a contact record is a free-text job title, not a permission | Holds permissions (Admin, HR, Financial and so on) and a history of membership roles over time |
| **Chapter membership** | One optional chapter, and only when **Enable Per-Chapter Contacts** is on | A chapter and a parent chapter, used across the whole platform |
| **Groups and teams** | No | Yes |
| **Membership fee** | **Never.** A fee payment records a member as its payer, and there is no route, import or setting that gives a contact fee status | Yes: fee validity dates, fee prices, renewals and reminders |
| **Other payments** | Yes: donations, event tickets, invoices, and even a recurring subscription | Yes |
| **Newsletter** | One flag with three states: subscribed, unsubscribed, or never asked | Per-topic subscription settings |
| **Event registration** | Yes, as an external attendee, with a QR badge and Event App access | Yes |
| **Company seats** | Yes, and the contact takes up a seat in the company's allowance | Yes |
| **Courses** | No. A contact cannot be enrolled | Yes |
| **Badges and points** | No. Gamification points are members-only (the Event App attendee badge, which is the QR pass, is a different thing and a contact does get one) | Yes |
| **Governance votes** | Only Event App polls. The admin-run governance vote is members-only | Yes |

<Note>
**The deciding question.** If the person needs fee status, courses, badges or governance
votes, they have to be a member. If they do not, a contact is enough, and it is cheaper to
maintain: no invitation, no password reset, no seat in your member count.
</Note>

<Warning>
There is no way to give a contact a membership fee. Marking a fee paid, importing fee rows
or editing the record will not do it. Someone who must pay a membership fee needs a member
account first: see [Becoming a member](/platform/contacts/becoming-a-member).
</Warning>

## What a contact can do

- **Pay you.** Donations, event tickets, invoices and recurring subscriptions all attach to
  a contact. [Payments and the fee boundary](/platform/contacts/payments)
- **Receive newsletters.** Contacts and members are drawn into the same audiences, and only
  subscribed contacts are included. [Newsletter and consent](/platform/contacts/newsletter)
- **Register for events.** A non-member attendee is a contact, holds the same QR badge, and
  can sign in to the Event App. [Events and the Event App](/platform/contacts/events)
- **Arrive on their own.** Contact forms, newsletter signups, public event registrations,
  donation checkouts, form submissions, signing links, imports and Stripe all create
  contacts. [Where contacts come from](/platform/contacts/sources)
- **Carry a profile.** Status, tags, custom fields, notes and an activity feed.
  [The contact record](/platform/contacts/record)
- **Sign documents.** A contract or e-document can be assigned to a contact, and the signing
  link can create the contact from the signer's own details. See
  [e-Documents](/platform/e-documents)
- **Trigger automations.** A contact is a workflow subject for the form-submitted and
  event-attended triggers, and can be emailed or tagged by a workflow. See
  [Workflows](/platform/workflows)
- **Become a member.** Merging is the real conversion, and it moves their history across.
  [Becoming a member](/platform/contacts/becoming-a-member)

Who may see and edit any of this is a separate question with a longer answer:
[Contact permissions](/platform/contacts/permissions).

## Turning the module on

**Settings** → **Modules** → **Contacts**. Requires `ADMIN_TENANT`.

| Setting | Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enable Contacts Module** | `contacts.isActive` | On | Turns contacts on and puts **Contacts** in the sidebar |
| **Enable Contact Statuses** | `contacts.hasStatuses` | On | Adds a status field to every contact and a **Contact Statuses** page where you define the list, its colours and its order |
| **Enable Per-Chapter Contacts** | `contacts.perChapterContacts` | Off | Lets a contact belong to a chapter, and adds the chapter filter and column. Needs the Local Centers feature on as well, and the switch stays disabled until it is |

Both extra switches stay disabled while **Enable Contacts Module** is off.

<Note>
**Enable Per-Chapter Contacts** does more than add a column. While it is off, chapter-level
managers cannot reach contacts at all, and only tenant-level managers see the list. Turning
it on is what opens contacts to chapter admins, scoped to their own chapters.
</Note>

## Where contacts live

**Contacts** sits in the **MANAGEMENT** section of the sidebar by default, next to Companies.
The entry opens the contacts list; each row opens a contact record, which is one scrolling
page rather than a set of tabs. Configuration is elsewhere, under
**Settings** → **Modules** → **Contacts**.

The sidebar entry is hidden unless you hold a contact permission and, on top of that, either
a tenant-level permission (`HR_TENANT`, `FINANCIAL_TENANT` or `ADMIN_TENANT`) or
**Enable Per-Chapter Contacts** is on. A chapter HR manager in an organisation that has not
turned per-chapter contacts on will not see **Contacts** in the sidebar at all, and that is
the expected behaviour rather than a permission fault. The sidebar itself is editable, so
your organisation may have moved or renamed the entry.

## How many contacts do we have

The nightly metrics job records `contacts_count` for the whole organisation only. It is
never written per chapter, because contacts have no chapter dimension in that job's model.
Two consequences:

- Contact growth per chapter cannot be read from the metrics tables or dashboards.
- Adding up the per-chapter rows to get an organisation total gives zero contacts, not the
  real number. Read the organisation-wide row instead.

Member headcounts never include contacts. Revenue totals are the opposite case and mix the
two, which matters when you divide one by the other: see
[Payments](/platform/contacts/payments).
