This walkthrough sets up organizational structure: a new local center (chapter, branch, regional office), the roles that run it, and the assignments that scope what each person can do. It uses the [LocalCenter](/api-reference/localcenter), [Unit](/api-reference/unit), [Role](/api-reference/role) and [UserRole](/api-reference/userrole) resources.

Who is this for: enterprise-onboarding integrations that build structure programmatically, federation tools that sync org-chart changes from an upstream system, and admin dashboards that audit permissions in bulk.

---

## Concepts

### Permission domains and scopes

Every permission is a pair: a **domain** (what area) and a **scope** (whose data).

| Domain | What it controls |
|---|---|
| `ADMIN` | Configuration: branding, modules, custom fields, settings |
| `HR` and `HR_ASSISTANT` | People: registration, profiles, applications, contracts, contacts. The assistant domain is the read-and-help subset |
| `FINANCIAL` | Money: products, prices, payments, invoices, refunds |
| `EVENT` | Events |
| `COMMUNICATION` | Newsletters and campaigns |

Each domain has three scopes: `_TENANT` (the whole organization), `_PARENT_LOCAL` (one chapter and every chapter below it) and `_LOCAL` (one chapter). So `HR_LOCAL` means "manages people for one chapter" and `FINANCIAL_TENANT` means "manages money for the whole organization". Some permissions imply others; the full matrix is in [Permissions](/platform/permissions).

`ADMIN_TENANT` satisfies every permission check. Granting it is **not** the same as making someone an Orgo administrator: the account flag that unlocks the configuration screens and several endpoints (such as creating roles and webhooks) is separate. See [Permissions](/platform/permissions).

### Where local centers fit

A `LocalCenter` is a kind of `Unit`. A unit is the generic grouping primitive: local centers, role groups (automatic membership by criteria), and groups. A role assignment attaches a person to a unit, which for chapter roles is the local center itself.

## Step 1: Create a local center

Creating a local center needs organization-wide HR rights.

```bash
curl -X POST https://acme.orgo.space/api/v1/local_centers \
  -H "Api-Token: $ORGO_API_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "Boston",
    "town": "/api/v1/towns/1",
    "officeAddress": "123 Beacon Street, Boston, MA 02108",
    "contactEmail": "boston@example.com",
    "contactPhone": "+1 617 555 0177",
    "contactFullName": "James Patterson",
    "isActive": true
  }'
```

`isActive: false` hides a center from new-member registration while keeping it visible to admins. Other fields include `parent` and `isParent` (hierarchy, step 6), `region`, `currency`, `website`, social URLs, bank account fields and `whoCanCreateEvents`. The response carries the integer `id` used in `/api/v1/local_centers/{id}` and, as a unit, in `/api/v1/units/{id}`.

## Step 2: Create a role

A role is a named position ("Chapter Lead", "Treasurer", "Outreach Coordinator") that can carry permissions. Create one per distinct responsibility. This needs an Orgo administrator.

```bash
curl -X POST https://acme.orgo.space/api/v1/roles \
  -H "Api-Token: $ORGO_API_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "Chapter Lead",
    "namePlural": "Chapter Leads",
    "isLocalCenter": true,
    "chartName": "Chapter Lead",
    "permissions": ["HR_LOCAL", "FINANCIAL_LOCAL", "ADMIN_LOCAL"]
  }'
```

`permissions` is the array of permission constants the role grants; they are the values documented in [Permissions](/platform/permissions). Roles live at the organization level and are reusable across centers. Other fields: `namePlural`, `unitType`, `position` (ordering), `isNational`, `isLocalCenterParent`, `isUserType` (the role is a member type), `isVolunteer`, `isNotEligibleForFee`. Changing a role's permissions later recalculates the permissions of the people who hold it (done inline for up to 300 holders, asynchronously for more).

## Step 3: Assign the role to a person, scoped to a unit

Scoping happens at assignment time. The same role can be assigned to different people at different units.

```bash
curl -X POST https://acme.orgo.space/api/v1/user_roles \
  -H "Api-Token: $ORGO_API_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "user": "/api/v1/users/42",
    "role": "/api/v1/roles/233",
    "unit": "/api/v1/units/4"
  }'
```

This makes James Patterson "Chapter Lead" for the Boston center only. Who may make an assignment depends on the unit: a local center needs HR rights over that center, a role group needs organization-wide HR rights or being a group admin, and a group needs a group admin. Setting `isAdmin` needs group admin or organization-wide HR rights. Other fields: `dateEnded` (end it on a date) and `localCenterPermission` (extra chapters, when the organization uses multi-center access).

Assignments stack. A person can be Chapter Lead in Boston, Outreach Coordinator in Manchester and Treasurer at the organization level: three separate `UserRole` records. The assignment also creates a follow relationship for the person.

To replace **all** of a person's roles in one call, send the full list to `PATCH /api/v1/users/{id}/update-roles` as `rawUserRoles` entries shaped `roleId_unitId` (and optionally `userType`). Orgo compares it with the person's active roles, adds and ends the difference, creates notifications, and recalculates permissions. It needs HR rights over the person's center.

## Step 4: Audit who has what

### Who holds roles at this center?

```bash
curl "https://acme.orgo.space/api/v1/user_roles?unit=/api/v1/units/4&exists[dateEnded]=false" \
  -H "Api-Token: $ORGO_API_TOKEN" \
  -H "Accept: application/ld+json"
```

The answer lists each assignment with the user, role and dates; `hydra:totalItems` gives the count. Without `exists[dateEnded]=false`, ended assignments are included too. Other filters: `role`, `role.isLocalCenter`, `role.isNational`, `user.status`.

### Every assignment for one person

```bash
curl "https://acme.orgo.space/api/v1/user_roles?user=/api/v1/users/42" \
  -H "Api-Token: $ORGO_API_TOKEN"
```

Useful for "show everything James can do" before revoking.

### The org chart

```bash
curl https://acme.orgo.space/api/v1/chart-members \
  -H "Api-Token: $ORGO_API_TOKEN"
```

Returns assignments shaped for an org chart, 100 per page, with the same filters as `user_roles`.

## Step 5: Revoke

Delete the assignment:

```bash
curl -X DELETE https://acme.orgo.space/api/v1/user_roles/199 \
  -H "Api-Token: $ORGO_API_TOKEN"
```

This needs HR rights over the unit's center (organization-wide HR bypasses the check). To keep history instead, `PATCH /api/v1/user_roles/{id}` with a `dateEnded`. The member's account is untouched; they only lose the permissions that role granted. A permission check is evaluated on each request, so revocation applies on the person's next request, but a session they already hold is not ended.

## Step 6: Restructure the hierarchy

For federations with regions ("Northeast Region" containing Boston, New York, Philadelphia), set a parent on each child center:

```bash
curl -X PATCH https://acme.orgo.space/api/v1/local_centers/4 \
  -H "Api-Token: $ORGO_API_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/merge-patch+json" \
  -d '{"parent": "/api/v1/local_centers/1"}'
```

Anyone holding a `_PARENT_LOCAL` permission anchored at the parent (Northeast Region) can then act on all centers below it, at any depth, without a `_LOCAL` grant on each. Deleting a center needs `ADMIN_TENANT`.

---

## Step 7: Self-service join for groups

Groups (not local centers) can be opened for members to join themselves. For a group marked joinable:

```bash
curl -X POST https://acme.orgo.space/api/v1/group/join \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MEMBER_JWT" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"unit": "/api/v1/units/12"}'
```

This is called as the member who is joining, with their session. It adds a plain membership (no role and no admin flag). A repeat answers `400 You are already part of this group`, and a group that is not joinable, or belongs to another organization, answers `400 You are not allowed to join the group`.

## Common gotchas

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The user has ADMIN_TENANT but a settings call still fails">
    `ADMIN_TENANT` passes every permission check, but configuration endpoints and screens (roles, webhooks, single sign-on) additionally require the **Orgo administrator** account flag, which is set separately on the member's Permissions tab. See [Permissions](/platform/permissions).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A local center cannot be removed">
    Deleting a center that still has active members or related data does not remove it: it is closed instead (status `CLOSED`, with an end date). A center with nothing attached is deleted for good. To fully wind one down, move members to another center first (`PATCH /api/v1/users/{id}` with a new `localCenter`).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Parent permissions don't cascade">
    Regional permissions follow the center hierarchy, so check that each child center has its `parent` set (step 6) and that the person's permission is `_PARENT_LOCAL`, anchored at the parent. A `_LOCAL` grant never cascades.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I give one role to every member of a chapter?">
    Two options. Automatic role groups (`POST /api/v1/role_groups`) add and remove members from a unit by criteria such as roles, region, age, gender, full-member status and fee level. Or loop on your side: page through `GET /api/v1/users?localCenter=4` and `POST` one `user_roles` per person. There is no bulk role-assignment endpoint, because each assignment has its own notifications and permission recalculation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
